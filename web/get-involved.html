Two docents built this. We're looking for the people who will carry it forward.

submarinedocent.org was started by Irving Greisman and Dwight Naset, two volunteer docents who wanted visitors' questions about WWII fleet submarines to get accurate, verified answers. It now has more than 190 verified FAQs, Medal of Honor histories, eternal patrol records, war patrol reports, and Ask a Docent, which answers visitors' questions in plain language.

We're both in our late seventies. We built this to outlast us, and that means finding people who care about it as much as we do.

Who we're looking for

  • Developers. The site runs on Python and FastAPI. The code is open source under the MIT license at github.com/submarinedocent/submarinedocent.
  • Researchers and editors. People who know submarine history, or know how to check sources, to review answers and add new material.
  • Museum liaisons. Someone to work with other museum submarines that want their own pages and FAQs.
  • A future steward. Eventually, one person or a small group to take over the project and keep it running for the long term.

What it takes

A few hours a month is a real help. You don't need a Navy background, just care and attention to accuracy.
